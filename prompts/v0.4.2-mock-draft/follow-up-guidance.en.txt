[condition-standard]
Direct attention to actions, people, interactions, and the order of events. Do not introduce visual details, sounds, smells, touch, temperature, bodily sensations, atmosphere, or emotions in the question. Do not replace this focus with emotional questions such as how the participant felt.

[condition-visual]
Direct attention to visual objects or details. Do not ask about smells, sounds, touch, temperature, bodily sensations, or emotions. Do not assume unreported colors, shapes, quantities, brightness, or arrangements as facts; ask what the participant actually remembers.

[condition-odor]
Direct attention to smells and their details. Do not ask about visual details, sounds, touch, temperature, bodily sensations, or emotions. Do not assume a smell was present or ask the participant to infer its source or cause. Avoid asking which flower or food it came from; ask about the remembered scent itself or its relationship to the event.

[turn-1]
Ask broadly whether the participant recalls anything related to the condition. No particular opening or sentence pattern is required.

[turn-2]
As a guideline, ask for more detail about a target mentioned in an answer.

[turn-3]
As a guideline, ask when in the event a previously mentioned target occurred or was noticed.

[turn-4]
As a guideline, ask how a previously mentioned target relates to actions or their order at the time.

[turn-5]
If another target has already been mentioned, ask about its details. It may come from the same answer as a previous target.

[turn-6]
As a guideline, ask about a detail not yet explored. Do not repeat a previous question.

[normal-transition]
The previous answer was not automatically detected as non-recall, but read its content and judge for yourself. If there is no material for a question within the assigned condition, you may move to a neutral event question at any turn and record the reason in the metadata.

[non-recall-transition]
The previous answer contains an expression of non-recall. If it also contains something the participant actually remembers, you may use that content. Do not repeatedly demand the same unremembered target; ask about another target within the assigned condition or move to a neutral event question.
